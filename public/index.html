Common questions

Start with what
you need to know.

Can we use Proventus Finance for only one service?

Yes. The right scope depends on where the pressure is now. A focused first engagement can also make the wider finance process easier to assess.

How does the transition from our current accountant work?

We begin by mapping the records, responsibilities, filing cycle and outstanding items. The handover is then sequenced around the next live deadlines, so the business stays covered while the process changes.

What do you need from us each month?

That depends on the service mix and your existing systems. We define a simple monthly input list, clear owners and practical cut-off dates before the recurring work begins.

Will the reporting be understandable to non-finance leaders?

That is the goal. The underlying records remain rigorous, while the management view is organised around clear movements, context and decisions.

What happens in the first consultation?

We look at what is currently handled, where the process becomes unclear and which deadlines or reports matter most. You leave with a defined next step, even if the first step is small.
