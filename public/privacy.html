Website information

Privacy, in plain language.

This website is designed to explain Proventus Finance and provide a direct route to start a conversation.

01

Information you choose to send

The website does not use an account system or accept document uploads. If you contact Proventus Finance by email, the details you include are used to understand and respond to your enquiry. Do not send payroll files, tax records or other sensitive financial documents through an initial email.

02

Website data

Basic technical information may be processed by the hosting provider to deliver the site securely and reliably. This can include IP address, browser type, requested page and request time. The website does not use advertising trackers.

03

External services

The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts and links to email software when you choose to contact us. Those services process requests under their own privacy terms.

04

Your choices

You may ask about the information held in relation to your enquiry, request a correction or ask for it to be deleted when there is no legal reason to retain it.
